DRSKit 2.1
Source: https://drumgizmo.org/kits/DRSKit/
Wiki: https://drumgizmo.org/wiki/doku.php?id=kits:drskit
Recorded by the DrumGizmo team with a kit lent by Jes Eiler of DRSDrums.
The kit is a kick, a snare, one rack tom, two floor toms, a Paiste Formula 602 hi-hat,
two crash cymbals, and a Paiste Formula 602 ride. Thirteen microphones, including
close mics, overheads, and room.

Rock, Funk, and Jazz are different dynamic levels and microphone balances of that same recording.
Rock keeps the close microphones forward. Funk keeps them tighter and a little brighter.
Jazz brings up the overheads and the room, with a lighter stroke and a long ride.
The snare bottom microphone is included so the snare wires stay audible.
Hits are not replaced with a drum machine and are not cut down to a click.
The cowbell is a separate CC0 agogo from Virtuosity Drums auxiliary percussion
(Versilian Studios and Karoryfer Samples); it is not part of the main kit.
